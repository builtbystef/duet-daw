# 0007 — A plugin position counts the producer's chain

Context: a track's plugin chain also holds plugins Duet puts there for its own reasons — the volume-and-pan fader and level meter every track is born with, the `AuxReturnPlugin` a send inserts at the head of a bus, and the `AuxSendPlugin` that feeds it — so a raw chain index made "add a reverb at position 0" on a send bus put the reverb in front of the return, where it processes silence and is never heard, with no error and no read-back that looks wrong. Decision: `addPlugin` and `reorderPlugin` count only the producer's plugins, clamped to the stretch of the chain between the return and the fader, and `TrackInfo::plugins` lists exactly that chain, so its index and the vocabulary's position are one index space. Reason: a Suggestion that says "put a reverb first in the chain" means first among the producer's effects, and the alternative — raw indices with Duet's own plugins made visible — would require the Collaborator to understand Duet's internals to place a plugin correctly (issue 6i7an7).
